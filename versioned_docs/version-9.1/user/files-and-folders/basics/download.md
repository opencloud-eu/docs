# Download Files and Folders

You can download files and folders from OpenCloud to your device.

- Choose the file(s) you want to download

  <img src={require("./img/download/choose-download-files.png").default} alt="Admin spaces" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

- Open the context menu - By right-clicking - or by clicking on the three dots next to the file.

  <img src={require("./img/download/download-options.png").default} alt="Download options" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

- Select “Download” from the menu or in the top bar.

- If you download several files or folders, these are saved as a ZIP file.
- Unzip the ZIP file on your device after downloading.
