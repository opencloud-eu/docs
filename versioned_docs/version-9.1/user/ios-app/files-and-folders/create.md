# Create Files and Folders

With the OpenCloud iOS App, you can create new folders and documents, take photos or videos, and scan paper documents directly on your iPhone or iPad.

- Tap the “+” button in the top right corner.

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/create-new.png").default} alt="Create new" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

Choose what you want to create from the menu:

## Create a Folder

- Tap "Create folder"
- Enter a name for your folder
- Tap "Done"

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/create-folder.png").default} alt="Create folder" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Create a Document (Requires Collabora)

If Collabora Online is enabled in your OpenCloud instance:

- Tap "New document"
- Choose the document type (text, spreadsheet, presentation)
- Enter a filename and confirm

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/choose-file-type.png").default} alt="Choose file type" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

The document will open in a built-in web view using Collabora for editing.
<img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/open-collabora.png").default} alt="Open Collabora" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>
<img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/edit-collabora.png").default} alt="Edit Collabora" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Take Photo or Video

- Select "Take photo or video" from the menu

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/select-take-photo.png").default} alt="select take photo or video" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

- Your device camera will open
- Capture a photo or video

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/take-photo.png").default} alt="Take Photo" width="185" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

- The media will be saved directly to your OpenCloud account

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/create/photo-saved.png").default} alt="Photo saved" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Scan Document

Use your iOS device to scan paper documents:

- Select "Scan document"
- Point the camera at your document
- OpenCloud will auto-detect the edges and scan it

You can then:

- Crop or rotate the scan
- Switch color modes (color, grayscale, black & white)
- Choose a file format (PDF, JPEG, PNG)
- Save as a single-page or multi-page file

The scanned document will be saved in your OpenCloud account.
