# Upload Files

With the OpenCloud Android App, you can upload files from your device or upload photos and videos directly from the camera.

## Open the Desired Folder

Navigate to the folder where you want to upload a file.

## Tap the “+” icon in the bottom right corner

and choose "Upload"
<img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/upload-android/upload-plus-button.png").default} alt="Plus Button" width="180" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Choose an Upload Option

From the menu, you can choose:

- "Upload" – upload a file from your device storage
- "Picture from camera" – upload a photo or video from smartphone or tablet camera

<img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/upload-android/upload-options.png").default} alt="Upload Options" width="180" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Confirm the Selection

Choose the file or media you want to upload. The upload will begin automatically.
