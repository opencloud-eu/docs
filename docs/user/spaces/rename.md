# Rename a Space in OpenCloud

You can rename an existing Space at any time to better reflect its purpose or content.

- Open the three-dot menu or right-click the Space in the Spaces overview, then select “Rename”.

  <img src={require("./img/rename/rename.png").default} alt="Rename a Space from its menu" width="1920"/>

- Enter the new name for the Space and select “Rename”.

  <img src={require("./img/rename/confirm.png").default} alt="Enter and confirm the new Space name" width="1920"/>

- The Space is now renamed.

  <img src={require("./img/rename/renamed-space.png").default} alt="The renamed Space in the Spaces overview" width="1920"/>
