# Upload Files

With the OpenCloud iOS App, you can upload files from the Files app or photos and videos from your photo library.

## Open the Desired Folder

Navigate to the folder where you want to upload a file.

## The "+" Icon

Tap the “+” icon in the top right corner

<img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/upload/upload-plus-button.png").default} alt="Plus Button" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Upload options

From the menu, you can choose an Upload option:

- "Upload file" – upload a file from your device's Files app
- "Upload from your photo library" – upload an existing photo or video from your media library

  <img src={require("../img/files-and-folders/upload/upload-options.png").default} alt="Upload Options" width="184" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"/>

## Confirm the Selection

Choose the file or media you want to upload. The upload will start automatically.
